{%- comment -%} Feed card for a `layout: directory` post (see _layouts/directory.html). It keeps an ordinary card's footprint -- same image slot, same grid cell, same featured treatment -- so the 2-column grid keeps its rhythm. What marks it as a directory is the image slot: instead of a photo it holds a folder drawn in dots, on the page's own colour, that is also a chart of the board -- a band per section, as tall as its share of the posts; bright dots published, faint ones not yet (extensions/dir-fetch.html) -- and under it which colour is which section. The body lists the sections, each with its count as a quiet number (no pills, like the board's column heads); a featured card also lists a few posts under each. Expects `post` and `post_item_class`, like views/pagination-item.html.
