{%- comment -%} Posting activity: posts per week as a line chart that scrolls sideways (drag with the mouse, swipe, or focus it and use the arrow keys). Opens on the most recent weeks; older weeks are off to the left. (The file keeps its old name because sidebar lists refer to it.) Styles: _sass/misc/sidebar-widgets.scss. Key off the FILENAME date (_posts/YYYY-MM-DD-title.md), not post.date. post.date renders in the build machine's timezone (UTC on Actions), so a post carrying an explicit time in front matter can slide a day. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign posting_dates = "" | split: "" -%} {%- for post in site.posts -%} {%- assign fname = post.path | split: "/" | last -%} {%- assign p = fname | split: "-" -%} {%- assign post_date = p[0] | append: "-" | append: p[1] | append: "-" | append: p[2] -%} {%- assign posting_dates = posting_dates | push: post_date -%} {%- endfor -%}
