{%- comment -%} Waline comments (settings: _data/waline.yml). Waline renders into the page -- no iframe -- so the site's own CSS styles it: _sass/misc/waline.scss maps its --waline-* variables onto the site tokens, which also makes it follow the light / dark switch on its own (hence `dark: false` below). Also fills the post-header badges (_includes/views/post-header.html): the comment count, and the most-picked reaction with its count. The thread is keyed by page.url; the badges use the same value. A terminal: the comments read as a log, "[yy.mm.dd hh:mm:ss] name > text", and the box under them is a prompt with a blinking cursor. Name only -- no e-mail or website, no Waline account login, no markdown help or preview, no bottom bar. All its text in English. not logged in an ASCII banner and "login as: _"; a name + Enter logs in logged in "name@cityofwonder:~$ _"; Enter sends (Shift+Enter: new line), "exit" / "logout" logs out; the emoji button at the end of the line unfolds a tray of the blog's emoji Waline shows times as "3 days ago", so the exact time of each comment is read from the comment data the box fetches (by comment id) and stamped on its card for the CSS to print. Replies come from the same prompt: a reply button highlights its comment and puts "@name " in the prompt; sending posts a reply (see "replies" below). The log shows them as a tree under their comment (├─ / └─), two levels deep. The log reads like a chat: newest thread at the bottom, by the prompt, older ones opened upward three at a time ("--More--" at the top); a long comment is folded to four lines with its own "--More--(33%)". Not logged in, a banner sits over the prompt (built here, .wl-banner): the "City of Wonder" logo, the how-to, and snow from November to February. Reactions: each gets its emoji (shown instead of the pictures when the window is a third of the screen wide or less: data-narrow on #waline) and its count in hex ("0x0A", from the decimal Waline writes). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign w = site.data.waline -%} {%- assign waline_ver = "3.16.2" -%}
